## Abstract
Classical univariate teleconnection metrics frequently fail to capture the non-linear, vertical, and sub-seasonal coupling between middle-stratospheric momentum deposition and lower-tropospheric boundary conditions across the Euro-Mediterranean domain. This repository contains the empirical database and the research paper documenting the SDI-BI-CI-EI Suite, an integrated multi-level diagnostic system standardized on a continuous, dimensionless metric spanning from minus four to plus four. 

The suite systematically decouples middle-stratospheric wave interference from near-surface continental advection across an uninterrupted historical matrix of 1,041 empirical records (January 1, 1940 - September 15, 2026) derived from ECMWF ERA5 reanalyses.

## Repository Contents
* SDI_BI_CI_EI_Suite_1940_2026.csv: The master climatological dataset containing 34 standardized parameters across 1,041 time steps.
* Decoupling_Stratospheric_Wave_Geometry_and_Eurasian_Blocking.pdf: The complete scientific manuscript documenting the epistemological framework, mathematical formulations, and paleoclimatic validations across 36 centuries of planetary history.

## Framework Axes
* Split-Displacement Index (SDI) at 10 hPa: Discriminates between Wave-1 off-pole displacements and Wave-2 resonant bifurcations.
* Buran Index (BI) at 850 hPa and surface levels: Evaluates the structural continuity of the Voejkov Bridge, continental source reservoir charging, and Mediterranean cyclonic draw.
* Christmas Index (CI): Resolves sub-seasonal wave-preconditioning during the late-December festive window.
* Easter Index (EI): Integrates an astronomical insolation correction derived from Gregorian Computus algorithms to evaluate late-season Arctic or continental outbreaks.
